TESTING OUR PROJECT IN BREADBORD!!!

Testing our Arduino project is an important step to make sure that all components are working properly. During this stage, we check if the sensors, LEDs, and other parts respond correctly based on the code we uploaded.

TRANSFER TO PCB!!

After successfully testing our Arduino project, the next step is transferring the circuit to a Printed Circuit Board (PCB). This process makes the project more organized, permanent, and durable compared to using a breadboard.

FINISHED PRODUCT!!!

We successfully completed our Arduino project by transferring the circuit from a breadboard to a PCB and ensuring that all components function properly. After assembling and soldering the parts, we conducted final testing to verify that the system works as expected.
